The ghostwriter

Not a rhyming dictionary.
A ghostwriter.

Rudy knows what a chorus is for. He knows a Country verse doesn't move like an Afrobeats verse, that a Gospel hook has to lift, and that a hook you can't remember isn't a hook. He writes to the craft of the genre you pick — then hands you the finished thing.

Three steps

How it works

That is the real app below, captured — not a mockup.

  1. 01

    Tell Rudy what it's about.

    In your words. A sentence is enough — a feeling, a memory, a name, the thing you never sent.

  2. 02

    Pick a language and a genre.

    English, Spanish, French or Portuguese. Pop to Gospel, Country to Afrobeats — each written to its own rules.

  3. 03

The craft

Everything a session writer would bring

01

Written natively in four languages

English, Spanish, French and Portuguese — written in the language, not translated into it. The idioms land because they were born there.

02

Every genre you'd actually want

Pop, Country, Hip-Hop, Gospel, Afrobeats, Bachata, Sertanejo, Chanson, Fado and dozens more — each with its own songwriting rules.

03

Real song structure

Verses, pre-chorus, hooks, bridges, ad-libs — laid out and labelled, the way a session writer would hand it to you.

04

Cover art, instantly

Every song gets artwork the moment it's written. It looks like a release before it is one.

05

Your discography, saved

Every song you write stays in your library. Come back, pick it up, take it further.

06

One tap into Suno or Udio

Lyrics formatted and ready. Hand them straight to the model that sings them.
