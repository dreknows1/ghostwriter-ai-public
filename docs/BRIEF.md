# SongGhost — Landing Page Brief

The front door for songghost.com. One page. Two doors out: the iOS app, or the web app.

## The forcing questions

**1. Who exactly is this for?**
Three archetypes, in priority order:

- **The gift-giver.** An anniversary, a wedding, a funeral, a 40th. They want a song *about
  a specific person* and they have a deadline. Highest intent, most emotional, most likely
  to pay.
- **The one carrying something.** Heartbreak, grief, the text they never sent. They aren't
  shopping for software; they're looking for a way to put it down.
- **The Suno/Udio creator.** They can already make audio. Their lyrics are the weak link —
  no structure, no hooks, generic phrasing. They need a ghostwriter, not a generator.

**2. What painful problem does it solve?**
"I know exactly how I feel and I cannot write it." The blank page wins. And when they try
a general chatbot they get a *poem*: no verse/chorus architecture, no hook that repeats, no
ad-libs, no sense of what a Country song does that an Afrobeats song doesn't — and, if
they don't speak English, an English idea awkwardly translated.

**3. What is the ONE core action?**
Type the feeling → get back a real, structured, singable song, in your language, in your
genre, with cover art, ready to take straight into Suno or Udio.

**4. Why this instead of what they do today?**
The honest alternatives are ChatGPT, Suno's own lyric box, and giving up. SongGhost's edge
is **craft**: genre-specific songwriting rules, real song architecture, and lyrics written
*natively* in Spanish, French or Portuguese rather than translated into them. Rudy is the
personification of that craft — a ghostwriter, not a text field.

**5. Smallest shippable version?**
Exactly this: one cinematic page that makes someone feel the promise in under ten seconds
and puts two unmissable doors in front of them. No pricing table, no blog, no FAQ sprawl.

**6. What would make us kill it?**
Shipping a beautiful page that breaks the web app at `/app`, or one that stutters on a
mid-range phone. Both are launch-blocking. The page exists to send traffic somewhere that
works.

## Positioning

> **Rudy is the ghostwriter. SongGhost is the studio.**

Not "AI song generator." The character carries the warmth; the craft carries the
credibility. Never lead with the technology.

## Voice

Warm, direct, a little wry. Short sentences. Second person. No exclamation marks, no
"unleash," "supercharge," "revolutionize," "harness the power of." Never call Rudy an
"assistant" or a "tool" — he's a ghostwriter with a personality.
